Methodology and date checked

Checked October 30, 2026. GameStop retail asking prices are not trade-in values. Public listing pages can change, and shipping or availability may need reconfirmation. The official estimator takes a PSA certificate number; GameStop's FAQ states the live pricing model is run when a card is scanned for trade at a participating store. Its stated maximum is $1,500 per card.

Market proxy formula: (grade-matched market reference − GameStop delivered retail ask) ÷ delivered retail ask. A recent sold record is preferred over a guide when available. These are gross price comparisons only; taxes, marketplace fees, resale shipping, grading costs, and time to sell are excluded. No acquisition receipt was available, so GameStop delivered retail price is only a hypothetical purchase-cost proxy.

Source discipline: PriceCharting PSA 10 guides may be estimates when grade-specific sales are sparse. TCGplayer prices refer to raw Near Mint cards and are never substituted for PSA slab values. Card Conduit's public service description is not a card-specific offer; no current Card Conduit buylist quote was verified. No restricted scraping was used.

Conclusion: No confirmed GameStop cash or store-credit offer was captured for any candidate. Actual trade-in spread, absolute trade-in profit, and trade-in ROI remain unverified for every card. The current best market-price screening lead is Cloud, Ex-SOLDIER #0202, but it is not a confirmed GameStop arbitrage.
