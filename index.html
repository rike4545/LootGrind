What qualifies for this list

  • Positive cash deal: verified GameStop cash payout must exceed the exact game's all-in acquisition cost, including sales tax, shipping, and other purchase costs.
  • Positive store-credit deal: shown separately and only when the credit's usable value to you exceeds acquisition cost. Store credit is never labeled cash profit.
  • Exact match: same platform, edition, region, disc/cart, completeness, and condition. Loose and complete-in-box prices are not interchangeable.
  • Fresh quote: quote must be current, linked to its source, and checked against the offer's expiry and in-store inspection requirements.
  • Ranked by real return: show all-in cost, cash payout, credit payout, net profit, and ROI. Pro bonuses are adjusted for membership cost when applicable.

Screening rule: net cash profit = verified cash trade-in − all-in acquisition cost. Cash ROI = net cash profit ÷ all-in acquisition cost. A game only appears as a cash opportunity when both net profit and ROI are positive. Credit opportunities use a separate, user-adjustable redemption value.

Methodology and date checked

Checked November 1, 2026. All values are USD. GameStop product-page prices are advertised selling prices, not trade-in quotes. Listed shipping is added only to create a hypothetical GameStop acquisition-cost proxy; tax, payment costs, and any later selling fees are excluded. Product inventory is not guaranteed unless the listing explicitly reports shipping availability.

Proxy ranking: ordered by PriceCharting PSA 10 guide less hypothetical GameStop listed price plus listed shipping: The Soul Stone non-foil +$43.02 (25.0%); Cloud, Ex-SOLDIER non-foil +$24.01 (22.7%); The Soul Stone foil +$40.87 (6.4%). The dollar gap and ROI are only market-guide comparisons, not a trade-in profit forecast. PriceCharting guide values are estimates derived from historic sales; grade-matched sample size and recency are noted per card.

Actual trade-in calculation: cash spread = confirmed GameStop cash offer − all-in acquisition cost; cash ROI = spread ÷ all-in acquisition cost. Store credit is calculated separately using the value you realistically expect to redeem; Pro's extra 5% is credit, not cash. Because no numeric GameStop cash or credit quote was captured for any candidate, all actual GameStop trade-in spreads, absolute profits, and ROI remain unverified.

Source limitations: GameStop's estimator requires a PSA certificate and its FAQ says the live quote is generated when a card is scanned for trade. No public static quote was available in the retrieved page content. Card Conduit's interactive price checker did not expose card-specific results in the public text snapshot. No automated scraping, restricted endpoint probing, or access-control bypass was used.
