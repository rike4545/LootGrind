Methodology and date checked

Checked October 29, 2026. GameStop product-page prices are retail asking prices, not buyback values. Several listings were last indexed weeks ago; listing price, shipping, and inventory can change. The estimator page accepts a PSA certificate number, but this research run did not produce a numeric offer. GameStop's FAQ says its trade quote is generated when the slab is scanned for trade.

Retail proxy formula: (PriceCharting PSA 10 guide − GameStop retail asking price including stated shipping) ÷ delivered retail asking price. Where shipping is not disclosed, the listed price is used and the limitation is stated. This is only a retail-to-guide comparison. It is not trade-in ROI, a guaranteed resale value, or a profit forecast.

Source discipline: PriceCharting guide values can be estimates when grade-specific sales are sparse or absent. TCGplayer references are for the exact raw printing and condition stated; they are not interchangeable with PSA slabs. Card Conduit's public service description explains its vendor-buylist aggregation and fees, but no card-specific public buylist quote was verified for these candidates. No restricted scraping was used.

Conclusion: GameStop trade-in value, actual trade-in spread, absolute trade-in profit, and trade-in ROI remain unverified for every card until a numeric cash or store-credit offer is captured and paired with a documented acquisition cost.
