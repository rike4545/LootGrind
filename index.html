What is actually verified?

  • Public market guide: PriceCharting provides grade-specific PSA 10 guides and visible sold-listing history; guide values can lag or be based on sparse sales.
  • GameStop retail: Product pages show public asking prices for individual slabs. Multiple PSA 10 copies of the same printing can have different asking prices.
  • GameStop trade-in: No numeric cash or store-credit quote was captured for these cards. GameStop says offers are generated by its model at trade time and only PSA grades 8, 9, and 10 are accepted. Use the official estimator and confirm the offer in store.
  • TCGplayer / Card Conduit: TCGplayer raw-card prices are not equivalent to PSA slab values. No comparable public Card Conduit buylist quote was verified for these graded candidates.

Methodology and date checked

Checked October 28, 2026. GameStop product pages for Soul Stone, Season of Weaving, Cloud, and Traveling Chocobo were indexed roughly three weeks to one month ago, so their asking prices and availability may be stale. Tifa's listing surfaced today. PriceCharting guide pages were crawled from two days to about a week ago, but some grade-specific sale records are older.

Proxy formula: (PriceCharting PSA 10 guide − GameStop retail asking price including listed shipping) ÷ GameStop delivered asking price. If shipping is unknown, the listed price is used and that limitation is stated. This proxy estimates a retail-to-guide gap only; it is not a buying profit forecast and never substitutes for a GameStop trade-in quote.

Sources: linked GameStop listings, PriceCharting guide and sold-history pages, and PSA certification records. No restricted scraping was used. All trade-in ROI and absolute trade-in profit remain unverified until an actual GameStop cash or credit offer is recorded.
