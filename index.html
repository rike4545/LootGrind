PSA values are for PSA-graded copies only and must match the exact card, printing, and PSA grade. Enter the PSA guide value manually after checking the official guide; LootGrind does not scrape PSA or claim an automated PSA feed. PSA values are kept separate from raw-card market prices and are excluded from raw-card spread calculations. Other prices are not automatically pulled from these websites by this static page; automatic updates require an authorized data provider/feed. GameStop's graded-card estimator is the relevant trade-in path for PSA-graded cards. Enter the value returned by GameStop as the GameStop trade-in quote; do not substitute PSA Price Guide value. LootGrind does not automatically query the estimator. Confirm the exact card, set, language, finish, PSA grade, and final eligibility/offer.

BUILT AROUND TRUST

A deal is only as good
as its data.

LootGrind is being built to make assumptions visible, not hide them behind a big green number.

✓

Source and timestamp

Retail prices and trade-in quotes should show where they came from and when they were verified.

A1
✓

Freshness warnings

Older quotes can go stale quickly. The app flags trade-in quotes older than seven days.

A2
✓

Exact match before ranking

Prefer exact UPC or model matches; verify condition, storage, region, and promotion eligibility.

A3
✓

No pretend live data

Product links are retailer searches, not live offers. Retail listings, inventory, exact variants, and trade-in values must be confirmed directly.
