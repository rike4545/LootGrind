How to read this snapshot

Date checked: October 27, 2026. The public pages have different crawl ages: Cloud about one month, Season of Weaving about two months, Traveling Chocobo yesterday, and The Soul Stone about one month. A page being accessible does not guarantee its listed item is available or its price is unchanged.

ROI status: No numeric GameStop quote was captured for any card. The official estimator requires a PSA certification number; GameStop explains that its trade pricing is generated by its model at trade time. Actual trade-in spread, absolute profit, and ROI are therefore unverified. Percentages shown are market-guide-versus-retail-listing comparisons only; sales tax, resale fees, shipping to a resale buyer, and other costs are excluded. They are not buyback quotes or profit forecasts.

Sources: GameStop public product listings and its official graded-card estimator; PriceCharting Magic price guides; linked PSA certification records. TCGplayer raw-card prices are not interchangeable with PSA 10 slab values. No restricted scraping is used here; this is a static, dated research snapshot.
